Guardrails in this setup

  • ✓
    Least-privilege scopes. The agent can read invoice and payment status and create drafts. It cannot issue refunds, change prices or touch account settings.
  • ✓
    No card data. It sees invoice and payment metadata only, never card numbers or credentials.
  • ✓
    Human approval. Payment retries and customer messages wait in a queue until someone approves them.
  • ✓
    Routed through your gateway. Tool calls go to one configurable gateway endpoint, so a policy layer such as Magertron can enforce its own rules, logs and spend limits. This path has not been tested against Magertron yet.
  • ✓
    Full audit trail. Every read, draft and decision is logged with a timestamp and who made it.
